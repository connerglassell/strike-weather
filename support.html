Common questions

The weather won't load.
Check your connection and pull down to refresh. If you're using Tomorrow.io or OpenWeather, check that your API key is saved (Settings → Weather Source). A brand-new OpenWeather key can take a couple of hours to start working.
Why is the radar blank?
Radar and warnings cover the United States. Lightning and satellite cover the Americas. Elsewhere those layers are empty.
Rain alerts arrive late or not at all.
Alerts are best effort. iOS decides when the app may check for rain in the background, so open the app now and then, and check that Background App Refresh and notifications are on for Strike Weather in the iPhone's Settings.
How do I use Tomorrow.io or OpenWeather?
In Settings, choose the source and follow the links to get a free API key, then paste it in. It's stored in your iPhone's Keychain only.
