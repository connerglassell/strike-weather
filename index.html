Strike Weather shows what's happening now and what's coming next: a live sky, a 24-hour chart, a week ahead, live radar with lightning and warnings, rain heads-ups, and a Time Machine that goes back to 1940.

No account. No ads. No analytics or tracking.
Your location is used only to fetch your forecast, and is sent only to the weather service you choose.
